How to play

🎮 Controls

Computer: WASD / arrow keys to move, E or Space to interact, F to bite, 1 & 2 for abilities, C to build, M to toggle map.

iPad / phone: Hold and drag the virtual joystick. Tap PICK UP / TALK when close to a target. Tap power buttons when unlocked. Tap ATTACK to bite predators. Open Colony to build your empire.

Move toward the pulsing gold circle to find your objective. Eat berries and mushrooms to heal.

🧬 How evolution works

Complete the queen's assignments to earn growth XP. Each stage requires increasingly more XP; 12 evolutions lead to the royal crown.

Each new evolution offers two mutations. Some improve speed or fighting; others boost gathering, healing, or commanding allies. Your selected mutation changes your abilities!

Skills spend energy and recover automatically. You can also battle beetles, spiders, and rival ants to earn experience.
